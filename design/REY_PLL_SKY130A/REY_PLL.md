
The loop. The phase frequency detector compares CK_REF with CK_FB, the
charge pump turns UP and DN into a current on the loop filter, and the
filter's voltage VCTRL steers the ring oscillator. CK_FB is CK/16, so CK
settles at 16 x 8 MHz = 128 MHz. REY_PLL_CTRL holds VCTRL at a supply when
the PLL is powered down or in one of the open loop modes, and kicks the
loop filter at power up.

The schematic is generated by `py/gen_pll.py`.
